1. `[0.__]` **Blameless Transparency:** Can participants report errors, risks, or bad news immediately without fear of retaliation or humiliation?

### 2. $N$ — Sovereign Agency, Accountability & Override Capability

### 3. $M$ — Semantic Clarity, Cognitive Coherence & Autonomy

* **Block Score $M = (M_1 + M_2 + M_3)/3 =$ `0.__`**

### 4. $O$ — Physical, Physiological & Material Sustainability

11. `[0.__]` **Fair Material Reciprocity:** Are compensation, unit economics, and resource buffers (`M.E.A.T.`) fair and resilient to shocks?
